## 26 Charlotte Street

OSM way [138339533](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/138339533), version 8, is tagged 26 Charlotte Street, four building levels, one roof level, white plaster and `double_saltbox`. The May 2022 Commons view visibly shows the pale frontage, three upper window bays, three arched ground-floor openings, dark green doors, railings, planters and number 26. The London Picture Archive's 1970 record 110408 independently identifies the wider 20-28 even Charlotte Street terrace and describes number 26's two doors, central window and classical entablature. Its inspected low-resolution preview supplies historical context only. Dormers, chimneys and the roof edge are visible in the 2022 view, but roof depth, metric height, rear/side geometry and 2026 condition are unknown.

## 28 Charlotte Street

OSM way [138339551](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/138339551), version 8, is tagged 28 Charlotte Street, four levels, brown brick. The May 2022 Commons view visibly shows the brown three-bay brick frontage, upper window rows, pale surrounds/brick heads, dark teal shopfront, separate door and railings. The London Picture Archive record independently describes number 28 at the far left of the 20-28 Charlotte Street terrace as Charlotte Laundry; Wikidata [Q66478891](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q66478891) also identifies 28 Charlotte Street and links the same Commons image. The inspected 1970 preview shows 28 in terrace context and a roofline/chimneys. Roof depth/profile, metric height, rear geometry and current shopfront remain unknown.

## 30 Charlotte Street

OSM way [138339531](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/138339531), version 7, is tagged 30 Charlotte Street, five levels, white plaster. Independent identity evidence comes from the Camden council planning record naming 30 Charlotte Street, W1T 2NG, and a [Fitzrovia News report](https://fitzrovianews.com/2022/02/15/licensing-application-lisboeta-30-charlotte-street/) that identifies its pictured ground-floor Lisboeta frontage at that address. The inspected 2022 article image shows a dark frontage, three tall openings, central door signage and railings. It does not show upper floors or roof, and capture day and reuse rights are unstated. The existing Commons “30 Charlotte Street” image remains a deliberately rejected fixture: its linked Historic England record 1379038 is 30 Tottenham Street, so it is not positive evidence. Upper facade, roof, height and rear geometry remain unresolved.
